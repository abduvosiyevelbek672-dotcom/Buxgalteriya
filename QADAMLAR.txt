APK ni kompyuterda dastur o'rnatmasdan olish:

1. github.com da bepul akkaunt oching.
2. Yangi repository yarating (New repository), nomi: buxgalteriya, Public.
3. "uploading an existing file" havolasini bosing va shu papkadagi BARCHA fayllarni
   (www, assets, package.json, capacitor.config.json) sudrab tashlang. Commit qiling.
4. .github/workflows/build.yml faylini qo'lda yarating:
   Add file > Create new file > nomiga   .github/workflows/build.yml   deb yozing,
   ichiga build.yml mazmunini ko'chirib qo'ying > Commit.
5. Repository'dagi "Actions" bo'limiga o'ting. Yig'ish 5-10 daqiqa oladi.
6. Tugagach (yashil belgi), ishga ochib, pastdagi Artifacts > Buxgalteriya-APK ni yuklab oling.
7. Zip ichidagi app-debug.apk ni telefonga yuboring va oching (Noma'lum manbalardan o'rnatishga ruxsat bering).
